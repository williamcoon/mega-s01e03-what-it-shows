Not one issue. Fixing the parser closes the parser reports only. The other two stay broken.

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants get day and month flipped: 7 April goes out as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type month first, as told, and the form reads the date day first. The date flips before the parser sees it, so the queued parser fix does not touch these.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day, not month for day. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” A month-first parser cannot do that; it looks like a time zone conversion. The desk already read these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json), but the cause table counts them under parser and footnotes them.

How they were counted

  • The export has overlapping pages: some reports come twice. One id, one report.
  • The cause comes from the tenant: its locale and its booking form (causes.ts). Intake’s intakeTag is a guess from the reporter’s browser and disagrees with the cause on many reports, so it is not used.
  • Every number on this page is computed from reports-4471.json at render time.
